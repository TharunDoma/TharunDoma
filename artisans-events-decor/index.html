Our Craft

Every Celebration, a Work of Art

At Artisans — Events & Beyond, we believe décor is storytelling. Temple-carved mandaps crowned with peacocks, elephants standing guard in bronze and gold, thousands of fresh marigold and jasmine strands tied by hand — every element is composed the way an artisan composes a raga: with patience, precision and devotion.

From the sacred geometry of a South Indian muhurtham to the glamour of a modern sangeet night, we design, build and style each stage from the ground up — built through the night and brought to life by morning.

How We Work

From Sketch to Shubh Muhurat

Listen

We begin with your story — traditions, colors, family rituals and the feeling you want guests to remember.

Design

Mood boards, mandap sketches and floral palettes tailored to your venue, culture and budget.

Craft

Structures are carved, garlands hand-tied and florals arranged fresh — often built through the night.

Reveal

You walk in to a finished dream. We handle styling, transitions between events and teardown.
